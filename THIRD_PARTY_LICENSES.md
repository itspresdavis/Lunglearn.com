# Third-party licenses

Application dependencies are installed from npm and kept under their own licenses: Next.js, React, Three.js, Stripe’s Node library, Zod, and the development tools. Run `npm ls` for the tree. No license key was purchased.

## Anatomical data

BodyParts3D, Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0. Credit line in `public/anatomy/ATTRIBUTION.txt`. See `ANATOMY_DATA_SOURCES.md` for the CC BY-SA header discrepancy.

The Human Reference Atlas female assembly was inspected as a candidate and not copied into the repository. It is CC BY 4.0.

Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA) and BioDigital (commercial) were not used.

## Reference material that is linked, not copied

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook and the May 2025 OEWS news release. Figures that appear on career pages were taken from pages fetched for this project. Allied-health handbook URLs are linked. This environment received HTTP 403 from BLS, including for the respiratory-therapist page, so those allied-health pages were not re-read on the day of the build. Wage numbers for those professions are not reprinted.
- NBRC public examination descriptions at https://www.nbrc.org/rt/ and https://www.nbrc.org/examinations/crt/. No examination item and no content-outline PDF was copied.
- CoARC program finder is linked. The directory was not copied. CoARC’s terms require written permission for commercial reuse.
- AARC’s August 2026 summary of RRT-entry states is cited as AARC’s summary, not as a statute.
- OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e is linked for anatomy and physiology teaching.
- DailyMed is linked from drug pages as a label search, not as a copied label.
- GOLD, NHLBI, and the journal DOIs cited on disease and formula pages are bibliographic links.

Questions, simulations, lesson prose, and interface copy in this repository were written for Lung Learn. They are not NBRC, Kettering, or textbook items.

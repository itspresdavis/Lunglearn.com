# Content accuracy

Lung Learn does not invent citations, reviewers, credentials, wages, pass rates, or clinical claims.

## What may be published

Career facts whose sources were opened and recorded, with the date and geography. Formula tools whose arithmetic is covered by unit tests and whose formula is shown to the reader. Anatomical identity records that state the BodyParts3D name, the FMA id, and the geometry note, without unsupported physiology.

## What may be read but not indexed

Clinical education stays in `clinical_review_required` until a named person with stated credentials reviews it. The page shows that state. Search engines are told not to index it. The sitemap omits it.

AI-written prose in this repository is a draft. Nothing in the editorial helper can mark a clinical draft published without a reviewer, and the admin screen has no button that does so.

## What the pages refuse to say

- A salary, job, or examination result is guaranteed.
- A typical finding happens in every patient.
- A calculator output is an order.
- A simulation predicts a real patient.
- A student ID without enrollment dates proves current enrollment.
- Lung Learn is endorsed by the NBRC, CoARC, or AARC.
- Continuing-education credit is available.
- Planned content counts as finished content.

## Corrections

Anyone can file `/report`. Reports land in the admin queue. Resolving a report records the action. It does not by itself rewrite a lesson. Taking a wrong clinical page down means changing its editorial state in source and redeploying. That limitation is stated on the editorial screen.

## Statistics

United States respiratory-therapist figures are stored with source URL, geography, period, and the verification date of November 23, 2026, America/New_York. The Outlook Handbook employment of about 142,000 and the OEWS employment of 139,790 are both kept, with a note that the programs count workers differently. Percentiles are not described as experience levels. No fifty-state wage table is published.
